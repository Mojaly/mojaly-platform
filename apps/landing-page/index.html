The problem today

Every corridor means starting over

Capital

A funded account sits idle with every bank and mobile-money partner you connect to.

Engineering

Time

Weeks of partner onboarding stand between you and the first payment in a new market.

How it works

Four steps, one integration

  1. 01

    Fund your account

    Fund a single liquidity account in the corridor where you're registered.

  2. 02

    Call one API

    Send a payment request. Mojaly quotes it and picks the eligible route.

  3. 03

    Mojaly routes it

    Rafiki and Open Payments handle it, or a hosted bank or mobile-money partner pays out.

  4. 04

    Settlement is recorded

    Your account settles directly with the partner. You get a webhook the moment it does.

✓

Mojaly never holds your funds

Mojaly finds the right payout partner and reports the obligation back to you. The obligation, and its settlement, is always between your liquidity account and the partner — Mojaly is the router and the record, never the custodian.
